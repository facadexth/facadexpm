**Source:** `แคตตาล็อคยูโรเมืองทอง2024.pdf`, 72 pages. It has a text layer, but the useful content is in the drawings. Data file: `muangthong-euro-mu-2024.json`. The maker is บริษัท เมืองทองอุตสาหกรรมอลูมิเนียม. **KC (เค.ซี.อินเตอร์เฟรม) appears on the dealer list (p72).** Every profile prints "WT x.xxx Kg./m." together with its dimensions, so all 99 dies have a kg/m read directly.

## Assembly rules, as drawn
- One sash die runs round all 4 sides, mitred at 45° with Corner Spigot (adjustable) and Auxiliary Spigot. The frame is joined at 45° or 90°.
- **Sliding:** the interlock has 2 options, which are printed (sash 42110 + 42298 core, or sash 42027). Two frames side by side join with 42111 (21.5 mm), giving the printed totals 126.9 / 164 / 201.1. Glass is held by inserts: 42790 for 6 mm, 42791 for 5 mm, the 42793/42663 sash variants for 12/25.4 mm.
- **Bi-fold:** 57.2 from the frame edge plus a 7 mm gap at head and jambs. 7 mm gaps at the hinges and the sill. The centre meeting pair 30061 adds up to 44.9. The estimated formulas in the JSON come from these numbers.
- **Hardware lists:** sliding is on p24-31 (anti-lift block, EPDM, spigots, rollers, drain valve, locking cover, keep plate, flush handle, bumper). Casement is on p52-53 (handle, keep plate, hinge, transmission rod, corner drive, pegstay). Folding is on p67, items A-K (top and bottom pivots, side pivot hinge, handle hinge set, CDQ20 transmitter, rod, door handle, lock, cylinder, cover plate).
- Neither cut formulas nor prices are printed.

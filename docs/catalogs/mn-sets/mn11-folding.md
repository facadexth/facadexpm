## Series
**I. ชุดบานเฟี้ยม** is the 101.6 system. It has 12 layouts, each shown as a profile page plus a drawing page (p4-27):
- one-side: 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 panels
- split: 1+3, 2+3, 1+5, 4+3, 3+5, 4+4 and 4+5

**II. ชุดบานเฟี้ยมนอก (Euro, FQ dies)** has 4 layouts (p28-35):
- 3+1
- 5 one-side
- 3+3
- 3+5

## Assembly rules
- **No cut formulas or glass formulas are printed in either series.**
- Each elevation labels which hinge or roller goes on each panel:
  - FH-01 x3 on the jamb hinge line
  - FH-02 / FH-02H / FH-02 between panels
  - FTT01 + FBT01 rollers on the carrying panels
- Euro gaps: 10 mm at the head, 4 mm at the sill, 6 mm at the lock stile. Depth is 74.5.

## Assembly rules
- **No cut formulas or glass formulas are printed in any of the three series.**
- **ULTRA SLIM:**
  - 4 mm gap between leaf and frame on 3 sides; 12.5 mm floor gap.
  - The bottom rail U7246 (75) houses the floor closer.
  - Mullions are U7114 (30).
  - Hanging layout: leaves meet at 20+20 with the U7373 pulls.
- **III:**
  - Gaps: 4 mm at the wall, 4 mm at the head, 16.5 mm frame-to-leaf offset.
  - Double doors have a 10 mm meeting gap.
  - Rails are U3225 (97.2).

# MN-30457/30458 fire-exit door with panic bar (assembly manual, 12 pages)
**Source:** `MN_12. ชุดคานผลักประตูหนีไฟ MN-30457,MN-30458.pdf`
**Data file:** `mn12-panic-30457.json`

## What it covers
Single leaf (p4-5) and double leaf (p6-7). **App types:** ประตูบานเปิด เดี่ยว/คู่, ประตูหนีไฟ.

## Profiles
- **Frames:** U7205 (used in the drawings) or M30018.
- **Leaf stiles:** M30457 (101.6x46) or M30458 (127x46).
- **Rails:** M3893 (101.6), U2514 (130) or M3397 (150).
- **Glass channel:** M30459.
- M30457, M30458 and M30459 are not in the 2021 catalogue, so their kg/m is null.

## Assembly rules
- **No cut formulas are printed.**
- The frame is on 3 sides with no sill. The leaf sits 6 mm above the floor.
- Wall gap 4 mm, head gap 4 mm, lock-side gap 10 mm, hinge-side gap 6.1 mm.

## Hardware
- closer 680
- hinge CZ-PH06
- panic exit device (no code printed)

## Pages to check, and what could not be read
- **Check:** p5, p7.
- **Could not be read:** none.

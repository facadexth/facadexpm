# MN balcony railing (assembly manual, 12 pages, has a text layer)
**Source:** `MN_15. ชุดราวกันตก.pdf`
**Data file:** `mn15-railing.json`

## Systems
1. **Baluster railing (p4-5).** Handrail BH035 (100x35), end post U7280 (60x38), intermediate post U7281 (60x45), balusters U7216 (30x15), slot cover U7218.
2. **Glass railing (p6-7).** Handrail BH034 (105x45), with the same posts and rails.

**App type:** ราวกันตก.

## Assembly rules
- Posts are at **1200 mm centres** (printed).
- The handrail runs over the posts.
- Horizontal U7280 rails run between the posts. The balusters or glass sit in the 16.5 slot, closed by U7218.
- Each post stands on a base plate with a 22 mm cover and an anchor bolt.
- **Not printed:** cut formulas, baluster spacing, glass size.

## Pages to check, and what could not be read
- **Check:** p5. The balusters there are labelled U276, which conflicts with the table's U7216.
- **Could not be read:** none.

# MN-7855 HEAVY DUTY 4"/5" swing door (assembly manual, 12 pages)
**Source:** `MN_10. ชุดประตูบานเปิด HEAVY DUTY 4,5 MN-7855.pdf`. The drawings are dated 07/07/2026. Page 7 still has "Lorem ipsum" placeholder text.
**Data file:** `mn10-door-hd-7855.json`

## What it covers
Single door (p4-5) and double door (p6-7). **App types:** ประตูบานเปิดเดี่ยว / คู่.

## Profiles
- D001: leaf, 125x52
- D002: frame, 101.6x64.45
- D003: astragal for double doors
- D004: small adaptor, not used in any drawing
- D005: threshold, 45x30
- M50625: bead (0.267 kg/m)

**The D-series is new.** It is not in the 2021 catalogue, so its kg/m is null.

## Assembly rules (from the drawings)
- **No cut formulas are printed.**
- Frame and leaf are both mitred.
- The threshold D005 is set into a floor recess. The leaf sits 5 mm above it.
- Gaps on the hinge side, from the wall: 4 mm, then the 44.45 frame, then 20 mm, then 15 mm.
- On a double door: 15 mm gap, 53.7 mm D003 astragal, 15 mm gap.

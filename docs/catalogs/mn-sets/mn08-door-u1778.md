# MN-1778 swing door with timber-look frame (assembly manual, 16 pages)
**Source:** `MN_8. ชุดประตูบานเปิด MN-U1778.pdf`. This is the MN/CENZA installation guide. It has no text layer.
**Data file:** `mn08-door-u1778.json`

## What it covers
One system, **MN-1778**: a 4" swing door whose frame looks like a timber frame. The manual shows four layouts:
- single door (p4-5)
- double door with multipoint lock (p6-7)
- single door + side light + transom (p8-9)
- double door + 2 side lights + transom (p10-11)

**App types:** ประตูบานเปิดเดี่ยว / คู่, and ประตูบานเปิด + ช่องแสง.

## Assembly rules (from the section drawings)
- **No cut formulas and no glass formulas are printed.**
- The frame is on 3 sides with no sill. The leaf sits 5 mm above the floor.
- Wall-to-frame gap is 4 mm (sealant). Leaf-to-frame-head gap is 4 mm.
- The rails sit between the stiles, as square butt joints. This is estimated from the elevation.
- On a double door the two M30037 meeting stiles have a 10 mm gap between them.
- Fixed lights are glazed straight into U1778/U6307 with U2256 + U3226. Under a side light, U1778 is also used as a sill.

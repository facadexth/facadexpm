# MN automatic sliding doors and general hanging-rail doors (assembly manual, 16 pages)
**Source:** `MN_14. ชุดประตูบานเลื่อน AUTOMATIC.pdf`
**Data file:** `mn14-sliding-auto-hanging.json`

## Series
**I. ชุดประตูรางแขวนทั่วไป**
- MN-U6359 hanging roller set (p4-5): 2 fixed + 2 sliding panels.
- DAIKEN semi-automatic single leaf (p6-7).

**II. ประตูบานเลื่อน AUTOMATIC MN-U3393**, using the HOKUYO DBSX-7 operator (p8-11):
- a 3-panel layout
- a 4-panel layout that parts in the centre

**App types:** ประตูบานเลื่อนรางแขวน, ประตูบานเลื่อนอัตโนมัติ.

## Profiles
- **Automatic:** U3393 leaf stile 101x46/48, U1971 rail 44x80, U1970 rail 44x51, U1719 jamb box, M3843 bead.
- **Hanging set:** p4 lists M40301, M42879, U5311, M40302, M41496, U6360, U6359, M42884 and U6357. But the drawing on p5 is labelled M30087/M30088/M30089/M30090/M4031. **This conflict needs checking.**

## Assembly rules
- **No cut formulas are printed.**
- Automatic, from p11:
  - 5 mm gap at the wall and 5 mm at the floor.
  - 4 mm centre gap between leaves.
  - The leaf stops 20 mm below the operator cover.
  - Depth is 150.2.
- Hanging set: 10 mm floor gap; centre stiles 90 + 90.

## Hardware
- **Hanging:** handle-lock 005-01/03/04, rail TLG121, roller TLG114
- **DAIKEN:** HCS-80 / HCS-8015 closer
- **Automatic:** HOKUYO DBSX-7

## Pages to check, and what could not be read
- **Check:** p4 vs p5 (the die mismatch), and p11.
- **Could not be read:** the dimensions on p7 and p9 are small and marked as estimated.

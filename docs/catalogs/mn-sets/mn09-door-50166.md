## Assembly rules (from the drawings)
- **No cut formulas are printed.**
- The leaf has mitred corners.
- There is no sill under the door. The leaf sits 4 mm above the floor.
- The wall gap is 4 mm.
- M3850 is also the sill under the fixed light.

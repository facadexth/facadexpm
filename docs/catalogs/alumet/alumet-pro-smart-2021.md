# Alumet PRO SMART catalogue 2021 (44 pages, scanned)

**What it is:** the original Pro Smart system (SM005/01-2021) for houses. Frames are 69.7 / 86.5 / 101.6 mm wide and 38 deep, wall 1.2 mm (bi-fold 1.5). Each profile prints **kg per 6.4 m bar as a min-max range** for anodize (สีชุบ) and powder (สีพ่น). 67 profiles. `kg_per_m` is derived from the anodize midpoint ÷ 6.4 (estimated).

| Series | App types | Frames | Spec |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sliding SS-01xx | บานเลื่อน, บานเลื่อนรางล่าง | 69.7/86.5/101.6, 2-rail; 101.6 3-rail; interior low track SS-0121/0125 | glass 5/6/8, groove 9.5, max door 2.2-2.4 m, max sash 1.0 m |
| Fixed SF-01xx | บานติดตาย | 86.5/101.6 | glass 5-10, groove 12.7, max 1.5 x 3.0 m |
| Casement SC-02xx (window only) | บานกระทุ้ง, บานเปิด | 86.5/101.6; can reuse sliding jamb SS-0129 as frame | max sash 0.8 x 1.2 m, all corners 45° |
| Bi-folding SB-01xx | บานเฟี้ยม | ext. 101.6, int. 52 | H up to 4 m, panel 0.6-0.8 m, ≤ 40 kg/panel; 45° cleat or 90° steel-rod corners |

**Formulas printed (p32):** aluminium weight = Σ(kg/bar ÷ 6.4 × cut length × qty). Glass weight = W(m) × H(m) × 2.5 × t(mm).
**Cut formulas:** not printed. p11 and p32 point to QR codes ("คู่มือตัดประกอบ สูตรตัด", "สูตรตัดลดบานเฟี้ยม").
**Hardware:** sliding SLM001/002/004/005/022/023, SLS018, DWM006/007 (p11). Bi-fold BFS001-BFS021 (p35).
**Pages to look at:** p6 and p18/24/32 (profile matrices: which die fits which frame size), p7-10, 19, 25-26, 33-34 (weights), p12-15, 20-21, 27-29, 36-41 (drawings).

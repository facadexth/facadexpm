## How prices were read
- Each table page was rendered at 200 dpi and read visually, row by row. The text layer was parsed by column position (the column lines are the same on every page) and every value was compared with the page image. All 26 table pages match.
- `bar_prices` hold retail THB **per bar, including VAT**. The 6 keys are: `พ่นขาว คอตตอนไวท์`, `พ่นดำ สเปซแบล็ค`, `พ่นเทา เมทัลลิค/กาแลคซี่`, `พ่นเทา แชมเปญเกรย์`, `ลายไม้ สักทอง/โอ๊คเดนมาร์ก`, `ตัวดิบ MILL`. A "-" is null ("made to order, ask for a price").
- `*` means "stocked in Galaxy grey". It is stripped from the number, and the column is listed in `stocked`. On pages 810-34..810-49 the mark is `@` ("stocked in Galaxy only") and is treated the same way.
- A wall thickness printed as "-" gives `wall_mm` null. The price book prints 1.0, 1.2, 1.5, 1.8, 2.0, 2.2, 3.0 and 3.1 mm.
- "ชุบขาว" (plated white) prices sit outside the 6 columns. They are kept in an extra `other_prices` key on 804-03, 810-06, 810-07 and 813-01..03.
- `width_mm`/`depth_mm` = the largest horizontal and largest vertical dimension printed on the drawing (mostly the 2026 overview text layer). This is "as drawn", not necessarily the wide face. About 45 values were set by hand from the drawings where the automatic match was ambiguous.
- `role_family` comes from keyword rules on the Thai name, using one vocabulary for all series (เฟรมบน/เฟรมล่าง/เฟรมข้าง, เฟรมบน-ล่าง, เฟรมบานตาย, เสากุญแจ/เสาเกี่ยว, ขวางบน-ล่าง, กรอบบานเลื่อน (เสา+ขวาง), ตบเสาเกี่ยว, ตบธรณี, ตบบานตาย, ฝาตบ, คิ้ว, บังใบ, กรอบบาน, กรอบนอก (วงกบ), ซอยกลาง, เสากลาง, เดือยเสากุญแจ, ชนกลาง, ฝาปิด, ตัวต่อราง, กรอบมุ้ง, ตบมุ้ง, กล่องเรียบ, ฉาก L …).

## Doubts
1. **No weights and no bar length** anywhere. kg_per_m is null and 6.4 m is assumed.
2. **802-23** bars per bundle is printed as **0**.
3. **804-22** name is printed "ร่อง 12. มม." (missing digit).
4. **Champagne-grey prices look odd** on 811-01..811-04 and 812-07/812-08: they are 15-60 % above Galaxy, against about 5 % elsewhere. They are kept as printed. 807-14 black = galaxy = 281, as printed.
5. **Page-810 header note:** "810-01..810-33 woodgrain and galaxy stocked at TM". It is not put into `stocked` (no per-cell mark). It is noted on each row.
6. **Picture conflicts:**
   - The 2026 overview drawings under 801-32/801-33 and 804-25/804-26 carry each other's dimensions compared with the price-book thumbnails, so the price-book thumbnails are used.
   - The overview drawings of 801-23 and 804-22/23/24 show different dimensions from the price-book thumbnails.
7. **Dies with no drawing in the catalogue or overview:** 810-22..810-26, 810-34..810-36, 810-38..810-41, 810-43..810-46, 810-49 and 812-14. Low-res price-book thumbnails are used for these. 804-13 is in the 2023 catalogue only.
8. **Dies drawn but not in the price book (not added as rows):**
   - 2023 catalogue: 801-15, 801-16, 801-17, 801-18, 802-10, 804-01, 804-04..804-10, 804-14, 805-04, 805-07, 811-05, 811-12. Also group 808 (808-06) appears on catalogue p.53.
   - 2026 overview: 801-34, 804-27, 804-28.
9. **Code gaps in the price book** (probably discontinued): 801-15..18, 802-10, 802-14, 803-12/13, 804-01..02/04..10/14, 805-04/07..09, 806-08, 807-03, 810-17/37/42/47/48, 811-05/12, 812-01..04/12.
10. **App types:** 810 and 813 have none. 810-05/810-12 (hanging-rail parts) could serve บานเลื่อนรางแขวน if wanted. 802 "3 ราง / ติดตาย" was given บานเลื่อน 3 บาน + บานติดตาย.

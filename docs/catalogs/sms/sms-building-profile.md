**Extracted:** 254 kg/m readings (one row per die × wall thickness) on pages 23–88. They come from the PDF text layer, and Vision OCR agrees with it. The Thai text layer was mis-encoded and has been decoded. Widths come from dimension labels on the drawings, so they are marked *estimated*.

**Assembly and cut rules:** the catalogue prints **no cut or deduction formulas**. Pages 95–102 are H/V section drawings with die call-outs only. They cover sliding door/window, awning, French door and bi-fold. Glazing pages 91–93 give:
- Sliding sash glass groove: B20027 adaptor is 23.2 mm inside / 26.0 mm outside, with a 12.8 mm rebate.
- Glazing-bead and gasket choice by glass thickness for 260 and 240.

**Pages to look at:**
- p.1–2: full die index per series. Some dies there have no weight, e.g. B10031, B20054–57, B22005.
- p.91–93: glazing.
- p.95–102: assembly sections.

**Not read:** nothing unreadable. Depth/width of complex dies is uncertain.

# Muangthong Aluminium: Euro Profile Catalogue 2024 (แคตตาล็อคยูโรเมืองทอง2024.pdf)

**What it is:** a 72-page catalogue for Muangthong's "Mμ Euro Series" window and door system. Every section is drawn full size with its overall dimensions, wall thickness and **weight in kg/m**. There are no prices, no standard bar lengths and no cut or glass formulas. The PDF page number equals the printed page number. Data file: `muangthong-euro-2024.json` (99 profile rows, all with a printed kg/m, 98 with a wall thickness).

**Summary table (p.70):**
- Wall thickness: 1.2-2.0 for Groups 1-3, 1.4-3.0 for the folding door, 1.4-1.5 for the hinged door.
- Glass: 6, 8, 10 or 12 mm, or 24 mm insulated.
- Hardware: Euro Groove (single or multi point).
- Corner joints: 45° or 90°.
- Only the sliding system has a built-in water tray and a 2000 Pa test.

**Pages a human should check:**

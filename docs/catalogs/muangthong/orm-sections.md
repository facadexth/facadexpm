# ORM Aluminium: section catalogue (แคตล็อคหน้าตัด ORM.pdf)

**What it is:** a 62-page catalogue from ORM / Great Aluminum, updated 01/02/2023 and certified to TIS 744-2563 / 284-2562. Each page shows section drawings with their overall sizes and a Thai name; only a few captions are in the text layer. Wall thickness is given as **1.2 / 1.5 (2.0 for the balustrade)**. There is **no kg/m, no price, no bar length and no cut formula**. Data file: `orm-sections.json` (142 profiles).

**How the members fit together (from the drawings):**
- **801:**
  - Frame: head 801-03, sill 801-01 (low) or 801-02 (high, with 801-14 cover), jambs 801-04 or 801-05.
  - Rails: **801-08 is used for both the top and bottom sash rails.**
  - Stiles: **801-07 is used for both the lock stile and the interlock**, which is made by snapping on 801-10 (ตบเสาเกี่ยว).
  - At the jamb, dowel 801-15 closes the stile; at a 4-panel centre, 801-16 meets 801-07.
- **804 euro:** sill 804-01 takes a removable roller rail 804-03. 804-06 / 804-14 serve as both stile and rail, and glazing adaptors 804-11 / 804-12 set the glass slot.
- **805 folding:** frames are top 805-01, side 805-02 and bottom 805-03 (or flush 805-07). The panel frame is 805-04, the mid rail 805-10 and the centre post 805-11.
- **No formulas:** only W and H are marked on the elevations.

**Could not read or not present:**
- No kg/m in the whole catalogue.
- Some die numbers are skipped in the sequence: 802-14, 803-12/13, 804-02, 805-08/09, 806-08, 807-03.
- The flat cover pieces (806-03, 806-05, 806-06, 811-09) have no printed depth.

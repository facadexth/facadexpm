# สูตรตัดลดบาน Smart Plus 2022 (RD Aluminium) — 18 pages, scanned

The supplier's own cut-deduction sheets: one page per window layout. Each has a profile key, a table of die no. / member / formula / pieces, the elevation and two section drawings. All 18 tables were read from enlarged crops. **210 formula rows in 19 tables.** Full data: `smart-plus-cut-formulas-2022.json`.

**How to read them:** W and H are the overall frame size. "หาร n" divides the whole expression: `W - 245 หาร 2` means (W − 245) / 2. Beads marked "ระยะตัดจากกรอบบาน / เสาบานเฟี้ยม / กรอบมุ้ง" are measured from the sash, panel or screen size (Hs/Ws). I checked this against the drawings: the H bead = sash − 2 × (sash face − 22) and the W bead = the H bead − 44. "วัดตัดตามระยะจริง" means there is no formula; measure on site.

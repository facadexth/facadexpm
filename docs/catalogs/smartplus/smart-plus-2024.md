**Assembly:** the catalogue shows joints only as drawings (detail A–E). The joint rules and deductions in the JSON come from the 2022 cut sheets.

**Mapping:** one die often fills two roles (15105 = เสาเกี่ยว + เสากุญแจ, 15106 = ขวางบน + ขวางล่าง). New role names are listed in `new_families`.

## 1. The system in one paragraph

Serie general is a **box-tube grid system**. Every outer frame member, mullion and transom is a box 101.6 deep (into the wall) × 44.5 face, called กล่อง. Glass is held either straight in the box's groove (กล่องร่อง) or by a snap-on cap (ฝาปิด / ฝาตบร่อง). Opening units are **sub-frames set into a cell of that grid**: a 2-track sliding frame (เฟรม 2 ราง), an awning inner frame (กรอบในบานกระทุ้ง), or a door leaf. They never stand on top of another frame. Two stacked or side-by-side units therefore **share one box member** between them (วงกบร่วมกัน). An adapter cap on the box makes the glazing seat or covers the groove on each side. SEEN on p2-p17 and p27-p43 (sections 9, 17, 22-30, 14-16, 59-65). This matches the owner's answer 3: for serie general, no add-on stands on an old frame, and every add-on becomes a shared frame plus an adapter.

## 3. Conventions per opening type

### 3.1 Elevation drawing conventions (all types)

- E1 SEEN (p15 A43, p42 IA6, p33 IA14): an elevation draws **one outer outline** and **one inner rectangle per cell** (the clear opening). Frame, mullion and transom bands are the empty strips between them. **No joint lines are drawn** where a band meets another band: the jamb band and the transom band merge into one strip.
- E2 SEEN (same pages): every band is dimensioned **50**, both the outer frame and every mullion and transom. There is **one** band between two units. There is never a 50 + 50 double band.
- E3 SEEN (p15 A43, p2 A1/A2, p3 A3): opening units are drawn as a **second rectangle inside the cell**: the sliding sub-frame, the awning sash and the door leaf. A sliding unit may span two grid columns: the mullion is interrupted in that row (p15 A43: 1600 = 50 + 725 + 50 + 725 + 50, and the sliding row is one 1500 cell).
- E4 SEEN: the dimension chain has three levels per axis.
  1. **Detail line, closest to the drawing.** Every band (50) and every clear cell (725, 950, 1400 …) along the axis.
  2. **Group line.** One segment per row or column. The shared band belongs to the first (top / left) group, and the last group also holds the far frame. p15 A43 heights: 1050 = 50 head + 950 + 50 transom; 1450 = 1400 + 50 transom; 600 = 550 + 50 sill. p37 IA21 widths: 1100 = 50 + 1000 + 50 mullion; 400 = 350 + 50 jamb. p31 / IA-series likewise.
  3. **Overall line.** For example 1600, or 3500 to the floor.
  The height of the sill above the floor (FFL) is also dimensioned (400, 1000, 1600 …).
- E5 SEEN: there is a plan strip above every elevation, showing the wall, the frame and the opening direction (door swing arcs, sliding arrows, "F." over fixed sashes).
- E6 SEEN: glass is marked with a "//" hatch in each pane. Frosted film (ติดฟิล์มฝ้า) zones are stippled.

### 3.2 Fixed (บานติดตาย), types A26, A26.1, A46, IA2-IA4, IA6, IA8, IA9, IA12

- The grid (SEEN p42 IA6, p10 A26) is made of jambs (1) (2) กล่องร่อง, head (10) กล่องร่อง, mullions (17) กล่องร่อง + ฝาตบร่อง, transoms (9) กล่องเปิด + ฝาปิด + ฝาตบร่อง, and the sill (11) กล่องเปิด + ฝาปิด.
- Butt joints between glass panes with no member (SEEN p40 IA2, p41 IA3 (66)): "ซิลิโคนใส", 5 mm gap. These are full-height panes joined by silicone.
- Which member runs through: the elevation does not show it (E1). By the owner's answers (rulebook U3d, U4) the jambs run through, head and sill butt, mullions run between head and sill, and transoms butt the mullions. Exception, SEEN p15 A43 and p14 A40-A42: when a row holds a sub-unit wider than one column, the mullions stop at that row. The transom above or below that unit must then run jamb to jamb, with the mullions butting it (INFERRED: physically, nothing else is possible).
- Glass, SEEN in the schedules. A-series: กระจกลามิเนตใส 3 มม. + ฟิล์มใส + กระจกใส 3 มม. IA-series: กระจกใส 6 มม. Frosted film or colour-backed glass is used on some panes (p33 IA13 กระจกพ่นสี, p30 IA6 กระจกโค้ทสี 4+4).
- Frame finish and thickness, SEEN. A-series: วงกบอลูมิเนียมพ่นสีดำ 1.5 มม. IA-series: วงกบอลูมิเนียมสีอบขาว 1.5 มม.

### 3.3 Awning (บานกระทุ้ง), types A1, A1.1, A2, A4-A7, A9, A11-A19, A21-A25, A27-A39, A45, A47-A54

- An awning sits in a cell of the grid as **inner frame + sash + bead**: กรอบใน + กรอบนอก + คิ้ว. SEEN p16 (3) (4) (14) (15).
- Sight line from the grid box face to the glass is **66 per side**. SEEN p16 (14) "66"; p2/p5 section 16 "66 44.5 66".
- Members around an awning cell (SEEN p16):
  - Head, when the awning is the top row: (12) กล่องร่อง.
  - Head under a transom (14): กล่องเปิด + ฝาปิด, with the inner frame screwed below.
  - Bottom over a fixed row (15): กล่องเรียบ + ฝาตบร่อง. The inner frame is screwed on top with สกรู 1.5".
  - Mullion between two awnings (16): กล่องเรียบ + ฝาตบเรียบ.
  - Mullion between an awning and a fixed pane (5) (6): กล่องร่อง / ฝาตบร่อง variants. INFERRED from the bubble placement on p5 A11 and p6 A13.
- Elevation symbol (SEEN p2-p17): a **dashed V with its apex at the top centre** of the sash, legs to the bottom corners. The sash is drawn as a rectangle inside the cell.
- Glass: laminated 3 + film + 3 (SEEN schedules).
- Sash: บานกระจกเฟรม อลูมิเนียมพ่นสีดำ หนา 2.1 มม. (SEEN schedules).
- Hardware per type, SEEN in the schedules: มือจับ VVP LW-45 สีดำ, แขนค้ำ VVP SA-10, บานพับ VVP FH-16 (FH-12 on p16 A50). A35 and A38 (small single awnings) list **no stay** ("–"). Counts per sash are not given (INFERRED: owner answer 12 keeps 2 stays per sash).
- Clearance: the sash is not sized "cell = sash". The cell minus 2 × (inner frame + sash sight, 66 total to the glass) gives the glass. The split between inner frame and sash is not dimensioned (question G3).

### 3.4 Sliding window (หน้าต่างบานเลื่อน), types A3, A8, A20, A40-A44, IA5, IA11

- A **4-sided 2-track sub-frame** (เฟรมข้าง/บน/ล่าง 2 ราง) is screwed into one grid cell. SEEN p15 (22) (23) (28) (30), p3 (22)-(28).
- Members around a sliding unit (SEEN):
  - Jamb (22) / (23): กล่องร่อง + เฟรมข้าง 2 ราง lying on its face. Sight line 44.5 + 19.
  - Head (28): the grid transom กล่องเปิด + ฝาปิด (the glass above sits in the cap), with เฟรมบน 2 ราง (27.5) screwed under it.
  - Sill (30): เฟรมล่าง 2 ราง (33; labelled "เฟรมบน 2 ราง" on the drawing, a drafting slip) screwed on a กล่องเรียบ, with ฝาตบร่อง underneath for the glass below.
  - Mullion between two sliding units (27): one กล่องร่อง, with a เฟรมข้าง 2 ราง on each face and ฝาตบร่อง.
  - Sliding unit next to a fixed pane (25): เสากุญแจ + เฟรมข้าง 2 ราง | กล่องร่อง | ฝาตบร่อง holding the fixed glass.
- Meeting stiles (26): **เสาเกี่ยว hooks onto เสาเกี่ยว**, each 34.4 face. The **lock stiles (เสากุญแจ) are at the jambs**. SEEN p15 (26), p3 (26), p14 (26), p41 IA5 (26). This matches owner answer 7.
- Elevation (SEEN p15 A43, p3 A3, p4 A8, p8 A20):
  - The cell is drawn with the sub-frame rectangle inside it, and each sash drawn as its glass rectangle.
  - The two glass rectangles are about one 50 band apart at the centre, with one line at the interlock.
  - A **half-arrow ("—\" pointing toward the closing direction)** is drawn in the sliding sash.
  - **"F."** is written in the bottom-right corner of the fixed sash when one sash is fixed (the plan strip says "F." too).
  - A3 / A8 / A20 are rows of 2-sash units, each one F + one sliding.
- Glass: laminated 3 + film + 3 (A-series) or clear 6 (IA-series). Sash: อลูมิเนียม 1.5 มม. Hardware: มือจับล็อค HUNZA, ลูกล้อ CENZA BR-100-S. SEEN schedules p3, p4, p8, p14, p15, p41, p43.

### 3.11 Combinations: how stacked and side-by-side units join (owner answer 3)

Every multi-row type in this set is a stack: fixed light over awning over fixed (A9, A13, A15 …), fixed over sliding over fixed (A3, A8, A20, A40-A44), and fixed light over a door (A5-A7, IA5-IA24). The join is always the same:

- J1 SEEN (p15 (28) (30), p16 (14) (15), p33 (9) (62)): **one shared grid member** separates the two units:
  - **Top unit glass over a lower unit:** กล่องเปิด + **ฝาปิด** (the cap is the glazing seat of the upper glass).
  - **Lower glass under an opening unit:** กล่องเรียบ + **ฝาตบร่อง** (the cover closes the groove and carries the gasket of the lower glass).
  - The opening unit's own sub-frame (sliding 2-track head/sill, awning inner frame, door Jackson box) is **screwed to that member**. It never stands on another frame.
- J2 SEEN (p3 (25) (27), p37 (17)): side by side, the shared member is one กล่องร่อง mullion. Each face takes either the opening unit's sub-frame side (เฟรมข้าง 2 ราง) or a ฝาตบร่อง for fixed glass.
- J3 SEEN (E2): on the elevation the joint is **one 50 band**, never two frames back to back.
- J4 SEEN (p15 A43 elevation and (1) (22) (23)):
  - The **outer jambs (กล่องร่อง) run the full height** of the whole stack, one continuous band from head to sill in every row.
  - The sliding sub-frame's side (เฟรมข้าง 2 ราง, a "side frame without box") lies on that jamb **only within the sliding cell's height**. This is how owner answer 5 should be read: the overlay side is as long as the sash unit's height, not the add-on's height.
- J5 INFERRED (from J4, owner answer 4): the head over a top add-on (ao_frame_far) and the shared transom (divider) butt between the long jambs. They are cut `len − 2·b(long jamb)`.
- J6 SEEN (E4): with the group dims of an add-on (owner's "1050 top / 1450 bottom"), **the shared band is counted in the upper unit's dimension**. Example: top light 1050 = 50 + 950 + 50; sliding row below 1450 = 1400 + 50 (its lower transom). Sideways, it is counted in the left unit's dimension.
